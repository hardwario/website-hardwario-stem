title: Expected Outcomes and Assessment Methods
---

## Students will be able to:

* Explain the concept of the Internet of Things
* Use the HARDWARIO IoT kit
* Program in Node-RED
* Create their own IoT project and present it

## Students will know:

* The benefits and risks of the Internet of Things
* The MQTT protocol for the Internet of Things
* Transmission technologies for the Internet of Things
* How to work with the HARDWARIO IoT kit

## Assessment methods:

* Knowledge test (comparing what students know before and after the lesson)
* 3-2-1 assessment
* Verbal assessment of the team projects by the whole group
