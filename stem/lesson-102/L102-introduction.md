The goal of the **HARDWARIO TOWER IoT Kit** lesson is to show, within a single class period, what can be done with an industrial kit for building Internet of Things devices. Students learn how the TOWER kit works, get to know its basic modules and learn to work in the Playground app.

## What will you need for the lesson?

* A computer with the [HARDWARIO Playground](https://github.com/hardwario/hardwario-playground/releases) application installed
* A projector or large monitor
* [Start Set](https://www.hardwario.store/p/start-set) from the HARDWARIO TOWER IoT kit
* Optionally, [other sets](https://www.hardwario.store/tower) from the HARDWARIO TOWER IoT kit
