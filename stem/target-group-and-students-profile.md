## Target group

* Pupils in the last three years of primary school
* Secondary school students

**Recommended number of students**: 9–15 for a seminar, up to 30 for a webinar

## Entry requirements

**Recommended age**: 12+

### The student:

* Is actively interested in new technologies and social issues
* Can use a computer and a smartphone
* Has a basic knowledge of English
